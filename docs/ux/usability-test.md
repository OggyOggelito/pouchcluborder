# Usability test — one page

A script for sitting with two or three colleagues, 20–25 minutes each. You do
not need a lab: a phone, the app, and somewhere they will not be interrupted.

**What you are testing is the app, not the person.** Say that out loud at the
start, and mean it. If someone gets stuck, that is the finding.

## Before you start

- Use a **real phone**, not a laptop. The whole app is built for 390×844 and a
  thumb.
- Pick people who match the roles: at least one **butikspersonal** (STAFF) and
  one **butiksansvarig** (OWNER). The two see genuinely different apps.
- Sign them in beforehand so the first minute is not about passwords.
- Have a second person take notes, or record the screen with their
  permission. You cannot run the session and write at the same time.

## How to run it

Read each task out loud, hand over the phone, and then **stay quiet**. The
silence is the method. Do not point, do not hint, do not say "it's under
Mer". Count to ten before you say anything at all.

If they are stuck after about 90 seconds, ask *"what would you expect to
happen?"*, note the answer, and move on. A failed task is worth more than a
rescued one.

## The eight tasks

Give them in this order. Skip the ones that do not apply to the person's role.

1. **(OWNER)** Lägg en beställning på tre produkter du väljer själv.
2. **(OWNER)** Beställ samma sak som förra gången.
3. **(OWNER)** Ta emot en leverans. *(There is no such feature. Watch where
   they look for it — that is the finding, and it tells you where it should go
   when it gets built.)*
4. **(alla)** En kund frågar efter något med lakritssmak. Hitta det.
5. **(alla)** Läs om varumärket Skruf.
6. **(STAFF)** När jobbar du nästa vecka?
7. **(alla)** Vad är det senaste som har hänt i butikerna?
8. **(ADMIN)** Finns det något som väntar på dig att granska?

## What to watch for

- **Where do they tap first?** The first tap is the honest one. If it is
  wrong, the label or the position is wrong.
- **Do they find "Mer"?** It holds most of the app. If people never open it,
  the bottom four are doing too much work.
- **Do they use the magnifier?** It is the fastest route to a product or a
  brand, and the easiest thing to miss.
- **Do they understand "stock"?** Watch for a pause at "3 stockar". There is a
  "?" next to it — does anyone press it?
- **Do they trust the "Granska" step,** or do they try to send from the
  picking screen?
- **On "Idag": does the screen answer "what do I need to do today?"** or does
  it read as a list of links?
- **Any moment of "wait, where am I?"** Note the screen. That is a missing
  heading or a missing back link.

## Five questions to ask afterwards

Ask these the same way every time, and write down their words rather than your
summary of them.

1. Om du skulle förklara appen för någon som börjar imorgon — vad är den till
   för?
2. Vad var krångligast idag?
3. Var det något du letade efter och inte hittade?
4. Vad skulle du helst vilja slippa göra i appen?
5. Finns det något du fortfarande gör i chatten eller på papper i stället för
   här? *(This is the most useful question on the page. Ask it last, when they
   are relaxed.)*

## Afterwards

Write up each session the same day, while you can still hear them. For every
task note **completed / completed with difficulty / not completed**, and the
first tap. Three people is enough to find the problems that matter: if two of
three hit the same wall, it is real.
